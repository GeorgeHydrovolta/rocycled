Deployment pathway

Validate before you scale. Always.

The SalinBloc™ pilot is not a product to sell — it is a risk-reduction instrument for the buyer. It generates a site-specific performance dataset that no specification sheet can replace. Scale-up is engineered from that data, not from projected figures.

01

Water analysis

Source chemistry reviewed. Technical fit assessed. GO / NO-GO determined before any hardware is specified.

02

Pilot design

SalinBloc™ configuration designed for your source. CAPEX/OPEX benchmarked against alternatives. Funding pathway identified.

03

On-site pilot

SalinBloc™ commissioned with full monitoring. Real operating dataset generated. Brine valorization potential evaluated in parallel.

04

Scale or expand

Full installation engineered from pilot data. Parallel SalinBloc™ units or engineered skid, depending on volume and configuration.

Funding note: SalinBloc™ pilots and full installations are structured through development bank financing, climate action programmes, bilateral aid, or industrial capital budgets. Hydrovolta has navigated all of these pathways in Belgium, Morocco, and Australia.
