Active deployments

Morocco — the integrated desalination and brine valorization reference.

The Morocco project is Hydrovolta’s flagship active deployment. It is the first commercial demonstration of the integrated SonixED™ desalination and downstream brine valorization within a single integrated project scope — validating both technologies in a real operating environment simultaneously.

Active Project · 2025–2028

Saline water desalination with integrated brine valorization

Seawater-intruded coastal aquifers at approximately 7,000 ppm TDS. SonixED™ delivers saline water desalination at high-recovery targets, producing clean water for both irrigation and drinking water supply. Residual concentrated brine is processed through the downstream valorization configuration for chemical recovery. Performance tracked through site-specific operating data.

Feed source

~7,000 ppm TDS seawater-intruded coastal aquifer

Primary output

Clean water for irrigation and drinking water supply

Brine valorization

Downstream brine valorization for chemical recovery

Funding

Flanders Climate Action Program

Utility pilot reference

Perth, Western Australia — Water Corporation nitrate and salinity.

Western Australia’s primary water utility contracted Hydrovolta for a paid pilot following 18 months of technical evaluation — Hydrovolta’s primary institutional reference for the water utility and government authority sector internationally.

Development pipeline

LOI and MOU-stage opportunities across South America and the Middle East.

These projects are at development stage. They are presented accurately by maturity and represent Hydrovolta’s commercial pipeline depth — not completed deployments.

LOI Signed
Brazil — South America

Remote groundwater remediation — nitrate and salinity

Inland groundwater exhibiting co-contamination of high salinity and agricultural nitrates at a remote site. High water recovery and minimal residual brine are central to the technical and economic case. Conventional approaches were evaluated and rejected on brine disposal grounds. Letter of Intent signed; full project in development.

  • Nitrate and salinity co-contamination
  • Remote site — autonomous cloud-monitored operation
  • High-recovery, low-brine configuration central to viability
MOU Signed · Pilot in Development
Saudi Arabia — Middle East

Selective ion recovery from deep brackish groundwater

Development-stage opportunity evaluating selective ion recovery from deep brackish groundwater and brine streams. The application targets SonixED™’s monovalent selectivity capability for specific ion extraction, with brine valorization and mineral recovery pathway under evaluation where brine composition supports it. MOU signed; pilot design in progress.

  • Deep brackish groundwater — selective ion targets
  • brine valorization and mineral recovery pathway under evaluation
  • Pilot design in progress
