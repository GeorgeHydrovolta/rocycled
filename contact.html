Engineering Assessment

Send the water data. We assess the fit.

Hydrovolta qualifies projects based on source water chemistry, target use, flow requirements, and site constraints. Submit the form below and our engineering team will assess whether SonixED™, a brine valorization pathway, or an integrated configuration is technically appropriate for your application — and tell you honestly if it is not.

No commercial obligation. Hydrovolta will not progress a project where SonixED™ or a brine valorization pathway is not the right technical fit.

?

What to send

TDS or salinity, pH, hardness (calcium, magnesium), nitrate, chloride, sulphate, silica, target flow, target output use, and discharge constraints. Brine analysis including ion composition for valorization pathways.

✓

What we assess

Technical fit, likely treatment route (SonixED™ only, brine valorization only, or integrated), pilot configuration, recovery targets, brine valorization feasibility, scale-up pathway, and relevant funding mechanisms for your geography.

×

What we avoid

We do not recommend SonixED™ or a brine valorization pathway when another approach is more appropriate for the application. Every assessment begins with an honest GO / NO-GO determination based on submitted water data.
