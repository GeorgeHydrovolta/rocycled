Technology

Two proven electrochemical platforms.

SonixED™ and the brine valorization process are rooted in the same electrochemical engineering discipline — developed through institutional research partnerships over eight years, with the process patent granted in 2021.

EDR

Electrodialysis Reversal

The foundation of SonixED™. Electric potential drives selected ions through membranes. Polarity reversal cleans membranes without chemical intervention, enabling stable operation across variable feed chemistry.

US

Ultrasound Integration

Hydrovolta’s 2021 patent. Acoustic cavitation at the membrane surface physically prevents calcium, magnesium, and silica scaling, reducing CIP frequency and extending membrane lifespan in validated operating contexts.

BV

Brine Valorization Process

Where residual brine has the right chemistry, Hydrovolta pilots chemical recovery using electromembrane separation and complementary technologies. Target products include acid, base, and mineral streams. Configuration is determined per brine chemistry.

Reference projects

Project experience across four continents.

References are clearly labeled by maturity. Hydrovolta demonstrates global momentum without misrepresenting pipeline-stage opportunities as completed operating plants.

Active Project

Morocco — Integrated Desalination & Brine Valorization

Seawater-intruded coastal aquifer at approximately 7,000 ppm TDS. SonixED™ delivers saline water desalination; downstream valorization processes residual brine for chemical recovery. Funded by the Flanders Climate Action Program. Hydrovolta’s primary integrated reference site.

Utility Pilot Reference

Perth, Australia — Water Corporation Nitrate & Salinity

Selective nitrate and salinity treatment across three public utility sites under a paid pilot contract with Water Corporation Perth. Cloud telemetry and regulatory monitoring active.

Brazil — Remote Groundwater Remediation

Development-stage opportunity for nitrate and salinity co-contamination at a remote site where high-recovery treatment and minimal brine disposal are central to the technical and economic case.
