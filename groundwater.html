The challenge

Designed for sources where conventional treatment reaches its limits.

Many brackish groundwater sources are commercially and technically viable — but not with conventional membrane systems. Silica fouling, variable salinity, high hardness, and brine disposal constraints each impose limits that compound in real-world operating conditions.

01

Brackish salinity

Target operating range for suitable sources is typically in the 1,500–20,000 ppm TDS range, subject to water analysis and project design. Recovery targets depend on source chemistry and configuration.

02

Scaling & fouling

Silica forms amorphous deposits that chemical cleaning cannot remove once established. Hard water calcium and magnesium precipitation progressively reduces membrane performance and lifespan. SonixED™ addresses both through acoustic cavitation.

03

Ion-specific contamination

Nitrate, sodium, fluoride, and chloride require targeted removal rather than total demineralisation. SonixED™’s custom membrane coating enables monovalent selectivity: target ions are removed; beneficial Ca²¹ and Mg²¹ are retained.

04

Brine disposal constraints

Residual brine from conventional RO treatment represents 15–25% of feed volume and requires disposal infrastructure. SonixED™ targets 2–5% residual brine in suitable applications, significantly reducing disposal burden and operational liability.

How SonixED™ solves it

Three integrated mechanisms. One validated platform.

SonixED™ addresses groundwater desalination challenges through a combination of electrodialysis reversal, ultrasound-assisted scaling control, and selective membrane configuration — each addressing a specific failure mode of conventional treatment:

  • EDR: ion-selective transport without hydraulic pressure. Stable output under variable feed TDS. Polarity reversal cleans membranes without chemical intervention.
  • Ultrasound: acoustic cavitation prevents silica and hard-water scaling at the membrane surface. 50–90% reduction in CIP cycles in validated contexts. Extended membrane lifespan.
  • Selective membrane: removes nitrate, sodium, fluoride, chloride while retaining calcium and magnesium. No post-treatment remineralisation required.
  • High recovery: up to 98% water recovery target in suitable applications. Residual brine 2–5% of input. Where brine chemistry supports it, brine valorization is added downstream.

Commercial pathway

Water analysis → pilot → scale.

Groundwater projects begin with chemistry, not hardware. Hydrovolta qualifies every project against source water data before designing a pilot configuration.

01

Water analysis

Submit TDS, ion profile, hardness, nitrate, silica, target flow, target use, and discharge constraints. We assess technical fit and tell you if SonixED™ is not the right answer.

02

Pilot design

Site-qualified SalinBloc™ pilot configuration designed around your specific water chemistry. CAPEX and OPEX benchmarked against alternative treatment options before any commitment.

03

On-site validation

SalinBloc™ deployed on-site with full cloud telemetry. Performance dataset generated across your actual water chemistry and operating conditions — the basis for all scale-up decisions.

04

Scale-up

Full modular deployment or engineered skid system designed from pilot data. Brine valorization pathway evaluated where chemistry supports it. Scale initiated when data supports the case.
