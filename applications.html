Target sectors

Six sectors where SonixED™ and integrated brine valorization address a specific operational need.

Hydrovolta’s strongest commercial opportunities share common characteristics: high value of water recovered, significant brine disposal liability, regulatory pressure on contaminant levels, or a brine stream with exploitable chemistry. The sectors below reflect where these conditions intersect with proven SonixED™ and brine valorization capability.

01

Municipal utilities & public authorities

Brackish or contaminated groundwater wells requiring salinity, nitrate, or hardness management. Hydrovolta’s selective ion removal addresses nitrate compliance to WHO and EU drinking water thresholds without full demineralisation. EU Groundwater Directive compliance deadline December 2027 creates regulatory urgency.

Operational driver: compliance, water security, lower brine burden.

02

Large-scale agriculture & irrigation

Saline irrigation water raises the sodium-adsorption-ratio, degrades soil structure, and reduces crop yield. SonixED™ removes sodium and excess TDS while retaining calcium and magnesium beneficial to soil chemistry. High recovery minimises water waste in arid agricultural geographies. Active Morocco reference.

Operational driver: crop yield, soil health, water availability.

03

Industrial process water

Food & beverage and pharmaceutical processes require consistent water quality specifications. When groundwater is the primary input, TDS variability and specific ion contamination create production risk. SonixED™ delivers configurable, consistent output quality with near-ZLD brine management to meet tightening discharge permit constraints.

Operational driver: product quality, discharge compliance, OPEX.

04

Mining & resource extraction

Mining operations generate high-volume process water and concentrated wastewater streams. SonixED™ addresses high-TDS and high-hardness treatment. downstream valorization adds a selective mineral recovery layer from concentrated streams where the brine chemistry supports it — reducing disposal volume and extracting recoverable mineral value.

Operational driver: discharge reduction, mineral recovery, regulatory compliance.

05

Desalination plant operators

Seawater RO operators and large brackish desalination plants produce concentrated brine streams that face increasing disposal pressure. Hydrovolta offers a pilot assessment pathway to evaluate conversion of concentrated brine into HCl, NaOH, Mg(OH)₂, and Ca(OH)₂ — eliminating disposal liability and generating industrial chemical products from a waste stream.

Operational driver: brine disposal cost, regulatory pressure, chemical revenue.

06

Remote communities & distributed sites

Distributed water treatment where central infrastructure or trucking is expensive and unreliable. SalinBloc™ containerised units deploy to remote sites with full cloud telemetry and autonomous operation. No specialist operator required on-site. Applicable to remote agricultural communities, islands, resorts, and construction site operations.

Operational driver: water security, deployment flexibility, autonomous operation.

Where SonixED™ and brine valorization perform best

The right conditions for maximum impact.

Hydrovolta’s technology performs best in conditions where conventional treatment has failed, is economically unviable, or creates downstream problems that offset its benefits. The strongest applications combine multiple of these factors simultaneously.

  • TDS in the 1,500–20,000 ppm range where conventional membrane recovery is limited
  • Silica or high hardness that accelerates fouling and shortens membrane lifespan
  • Nitrate, sodium, or fluoride requiring selective removal rather than total demineralisation
  • Brine disposal constraints: restricted discharge permits, inland sites, or tightening regulations
  • Brine chemistry containing NaCl, MgCl₂, or CaCl₂ at concentrations supporting brine valorization
  • Sites requiring pilot validation before large capital commitment
