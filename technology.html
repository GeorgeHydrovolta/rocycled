SonixED™

Electrodialysis reversal strengthened by ultrasound-assisted scaling control.

SonixED™ integrates three distinct engineering innovations into one automated platform. Each component addresses a specific failure mode of conventional membrane treatment in the 1,500–20,000 ppm TDS brackish range.

01

Electrodialysis Reversal (EDR)

An electric potential drives dissolved ions through ion-exchange membranes. Unlike reverse osmosis, water does not pass through the membrane — ions do. Periodic polarity reversal cleans membranes in-situ without chemical intervention, enabling stable performance across variable feed salinity.

  • Ion-selective transport without hydraulic pressure
  • Stable output under variable TDS feed conditions
  • Polarity reversal eliminates chemical CIP dependency
02

Ultrasound Integration (Patent 2021)

Hydrovolta’s granted patent integrates ultrasound transducers directly at the membrane surface. Acoustic cavitation continuously disrupts scale nucleation, prevents silica fouling, and improves ion transport efficiency — addressing the primary failure mode of hard-water and silica-laden sources that defeats conventional membrane systems.

  • Acoustic cavitation prevents calcium, magnesium and silica scale
  • 50–90% reduction in CIP cycles in validated operating contexts
  • Extended membrane operational lifespan
03

Custom Membrane Coating

A proprietary membrane coating enables monovalent ion selectivity. The system is configured to remove nitrate (NO₃¯), sodium (Na¹), chloride (Cl¯), and fluoride (F¯) while retaining beneficial calcium (Ca²¹) and magnesium (Mg²¹). Output meets drinking water and agricultural standards without full demineralisation or downstream remineralisation cost.

  • Selective nitrate removal — validated at Water Corporation Perth (3 utility sites)
  • Mineral-balanced output — no post-treatment remineralisation required
  • Configurable per application ion targets
Brine valorization

Chemical recovery from concentrated brine where the chemistry supports it.

Hydrovolta’s brine valorization capability evaluates and pilots chemical recovery from concentrated streams generated by SonixED™ desalination or existing desalination operations. The specific process configuration — which may combine electromembrane separation, selective membranes, and complementary technologies — is determined by brine composition analysis and pilot assessment. Confirmed core to Hydrovolta’s R&D portfolio and active alongside SonixED™ desalination in the Morocco integrated deployment.

How feasibility is determined

Not every brine stream supports valorization. Hydrovolta assesses brine composition, ion concentrations, flow volume, and site constraints to determine whether a chemical recovery pilot is technically and commercially viable. Where it is, the process configuration is specified and piloted before any scale-up commitment.

  • Brine composition analysis: ion profile, TDS, pH, temperature
  • Target product feasibility assessment based on chemistry
  • Process configuration selected per brine type and target outputs
  • Pilot deployment generates the dataset for scale-up decisions

The specific electromembrane and separation technologies used are configured to the brine chemistry of each project. Hydrovolta does not apply a single fixed process architecture across all applications.

Potential recoverable product streams

Target products are subject to brine composition and pilot results. The streams below represent recovery potential for typical NaCl-rich and divalent-rich brine chemistries.

HCl

Hydrochloric acid. For chemical processing, metal treatment, and pH control applications where brine chloride concentration supports recovery.

NaOH

Sodium hydroxide. For chemical manufacturing, water treatment, and industrial processes where sodium-rich brine composition is suitable.

Mg(OH)₂

Magnesium hydroxide. Recoverable from magnesium-rich brine streams for flame retardant, pharmaceutical, and neutralisation uses.

Ca(OH)₂

Calcium hydroxide. Recoverable from calcium-rich brine streams for construction, agriculture, and water treatment applications.

ZLD pathway

Towards zero liquid discharge through integrated operation.

SonixED™ achieves up to 98% water recovery from the feed source, reducing residual brine to 2–5% of input volume. Downstream valorization converts that concentrated brine into recoverable chemical streams where the brine chemistry supports it. The integrated operation approaches zero liquid discharge: most water is recovered as clean product; most of the brine is converted to saleable chemicals; net waste volume is minimised.

01

Feed water in

Brackish or saline groundwater at 1,500–20,000 ppm TDS enters SonixED™.

02

SonixED™ desalination

Up to 98% recovered as clean product water. Residual brine concentrated to 2–5% of input volume.

03

Brine valorization

Concentrated brine enters the valorization configuration. Where brine chemistry supports it, the process converts the stream into recoverable chemical products including acid, base, and mineral fractions.

04

Near-ZLD output

Product water delivered. Industrial chemicals recovered. Net liquid waste minimised. Active in Morocco.
